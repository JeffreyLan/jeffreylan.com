JOS

A personal cognitive operating system.

JOS does not try to remember the world. It remembers the definitions, decisions, principles and identity that reality has validated over time — the ones that actually shape a person.

JOS 不試圖記住世界。JOS 試圖記住那些被現實長期驗證後，最終塑造了一個人的定義、決策、原則與身份。

The question

Not “how can AI remember more?” but “what is worth remembering?”

Language models are stateless. Most attempts to fix this add memory: retrieval, vector stores, longer context. JOS starts from a different question — which definitions made me who I am today, and which of them still hold?

Whoever holds the power to define is the one who stays human. JOS is built to keep that power with the person, not the model.

Principles

The model is the CPU. The record is the self.

The LLM is a compute engine

Claude is the engine JOS runs on today. Any model can be swapped in; who JOS is does not change.

Identity lives in the record

Principles and decision history stay in a local, auditable, editable store — never in model weights.

Context construction is the product

Every call rebuilds context from the record. The value is deciding what deserves to enter it.

A decision requires an artifact

A decision is a commitment, proven by something shipped into the world — not a thought.

Ontology v1

Eight entities, from fact to self.

  1. ObservationA fact. No interpretation allowed.
  2. InquiryThe question a fact raises — refined in a loop until it collapses into will.
  3. PositionWhere you are in life right now.
  4. EnergyYour real capacity to act.
  5. DecisionA commitment, with an artifact.
  6. PatternWhat repeats across decisions.
  7. PrincipleA definition that keeps surviving reality.
  8. IdentityThe result, not the starting point.

Fast action loop

Observation → Inquiry → Position → Energy

Slow wisdom loop

Decision → Pattern → Principle → Identity

How JOS runs

An autonomous system that knows its place.

  • Timers give JOS initiative

    JOS does not wait to be asked. Independent timers drive what it observes and when it considers speaking.

  • Time itself is observed

    Every half hour JOS casts an I Ching hexagram using a simulated yarrow-stalk method. The cast is recorded as fact; any reading of it is kept separate, as interpretation.

  • A voice that does not overstep

    JOS asks and reflects. It never decides for the person — the commitment, and its consequences, stay human.
