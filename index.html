The thesis

Not “how can AI remember more?” but “what is worth remembering?”

01

Models are stateless

Every call starts from zero. Retrieval and memory layers try to fix this by storing more.

02

Memory is not identity

A pile of past conversations is not a self. What shapes a person is a small set of definitions that keep surviving reality.

03

Definition is the power

Whoever holds the power to define stays human. JOS keeps that power with the person, not the model.

Principles

The model is the CPU.
The record is the self.

Four commitments shape every part of the system.

The LLM is a compute engine

Claude is the engine JOS runs on today. Any model can be swapped in; who JOS is does not change.

Identity lives in the record

Principles and decision history stay in a local, auditable, editable store — never in model weights.

Context construction is the product

Every call rebuilds context from the record. The value is deciding what deserves to enter it.

A decision requires an artifact

A decision is a commitment, proven by something shipped into the world — not a thought.

Engine

Continuity

Your record

Local, append-only, human-readable. Genesis, observations, decisions, principles.

i.

Time itself is observed

Every half hour JOS casts an I Ching hexagram with a simulated yarrow-stalk method. The cast is recorded as fact; any reading of it is kept separate, as interpretation.

ii.

Silence by default

Most moments do not deserve an interruption. JOS speaks only when an observation is worth stopping for — and almost never late at night.

iii.

A voice that does not overstep

JOS asks and reflects. It never decides for you; the commitment, and its consequences, stay human.

First product

JAPP — a diary that installs a JOS.

It looks like a journal. Write the way you always have; JOS keeps time, observes, and reflects back what you have actually committed to.

  • Starts with its own genesis: a cast at the moment you first open it.
  • Separates what happened from what you think it means.
  • Turns commitments into decisions with artifacts, and patterns into principles you can test.
  • Apple Watch integration planned, so the system can sense the body as well as the page.
